The files in this folder are a slice of English OpenList
(https://huggingface.co/datasets/ryanjosephkamp/english-openlist), taken from its
per-length word files of the release dated September 24, 2026. Each file holds,
on its first line, the words a round here can deal as answers and, on its second,
after a marker for this repository's spelling check, the rest of the words a
round accepts as guesses. Words keep the spellings English OpenList gives them. Files 2 to 5 hold every valid
word of that length; longer files hold about a thousand.
