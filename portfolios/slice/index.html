Series 1 · 2026 to present

Splashery

An interactive Gaussian splat playground that runs entirely in the browser. Pick a toy (a real captured object, or one generated from a seed), spin it, poke it, blow on it, splash paint on it, drop it, watch it fall apart and come back, make your own, and share it as a link, an embed, a GIF or a video.

Play Code Tinkerer's Manual

Ryan defined the project's vision and its toy categories, refined the interaction design through hands-on testing, and contributed mathematical recipes.

A · THE VOLUME VIEWER

Open a scan of your own

The volume viewer opens a CT, MRI or microscope scan (DICOM, NIfTI, a TIFF stack or a raw volume) on your device, so nothing is uploaded, and draws it as splats. It has window and level with presets for bone, soft tissue and the full range, a transfer function for colors and opacity, a cut plane on each axis that a drag moves, a thin-slice view, and a maximum-intensity picture.

Its samples are real scans of things that aren't people: the walnut and the young gar's head on the box above. The viewer sits behind Splashery's labs switch.

B · HOW A CT SEES

Toys that show how the machines see

A CT ring sweeps along a nautilus shell and builds its volume slice by slice, then a drag cuts into it. A kiwi's or an orange's MRI slices scroll under a drag. Bags ride a belt through an airport scanner. Pollen, diatoms and a snowflake appear as a scanning electron microscope sees them, and a mug of tea cools in a thermal camera's false colors.

On this page: the slice you chose, rebuilt from nothing but its own X-ray shadows by filtered back projection. One view is a smear. Slide toward 180.

C · THE ANATOMY ATLAS

A body, peeled layer by layer

A standing figure about 1.8 m tall, peeled like a clinical atlas. A tap opens the outer layer along its seams and lifts it away; after the organs, a tap brings every layer back in order. A labels switch lists the parts and highlights the one you tapped.

Four films stacked on the box. Click the top one to peel it.

D · THE REAL THING

The same walnut, in Splashery

Everything above is this page's own drawing of the scan. This one is Splashery's: its walnut toy in the embed player, the same CT scan drawn as volume splats. It turns when idle and you can orbit it. In the playground you can cut it, or show only its dense shell.

2 · 2026 TO PRESENT

grooph

Visual loop graphs for coding-agent workflows. You draw who builds, who checks, where a person decides and when the work stops. grooph checks that every loop names a stop, then compiles the graph into a prompt package for a separate coding-agent harness. It runs no agent and calls no model, and graphs stay on your device.
