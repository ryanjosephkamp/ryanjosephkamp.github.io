Take one home

Every portfolio here is open source under the MIT license. Download one, put your own name and work in it, and publish it anywhere that serves static files.

Fonts, scans, maps and word lists inside a template keep their own licenses, and each template lists them. The source of all eleven is on GitHub.

  1. Download it

    Choose Template under any poster. You get a zip of that site, ready to open.

  2. Make it yours

    TEMPLATE.md lists everything to replace: the name, the bio, the projects, the links and the social card.

  3. Keep the look

    DESIGN.md is the site's design system: its colors, type, motion and layout. Give it to Claude Code, or any coding agent, and ask it to follow it.
