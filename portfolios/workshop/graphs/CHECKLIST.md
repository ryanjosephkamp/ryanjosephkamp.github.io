# Checklist for a drawing on sheet 1

The bar the critic holds a draft to. `node concepts/workshop/tools/measure.mjs` prints the numbers
behind each item.

1. The splats on the sheet total the budget, 640. None are lost to rounding.
2. Each part's share of the budget is within one splat of its share by surface area.
3. No region of the toy is more than 1.15 times as dense as another, in splats per unit area.
