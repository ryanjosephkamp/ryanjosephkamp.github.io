Splashery

A 3D Gaussian splat engine and studio, in a browser tab.

Everything on this page is drawn from splats: soft, colored 3D Gaussians, sorted by depth and blended on your device. This one is a real capture of a grape. Drag it sideways to take one frame apart.

sort · project · blend

One frame, three steps

A worker thread sorts every splat by its depth along the camera's line of sight. The graphics chip turns each 3D Gaussian into a 2D ellipse on the screen. Then the ellipses are blended from the farthest to the nearest, each one covering what is behind it by its opacity.

Scrub the blend to build the frame from the back, and switch passes to see what each splat carries.

μ · Σ · color · α

One splat

A splat is a 3D Gaussian: a center μ, a covariance Σ = R S Sᵀ Rᵀ built from three scales and a rotation, a color, and an opacity α. On the screen it becomes a 2D Gaussian with covariance J W Σ Wᵀ Jᵀ, and that ellipse is what gets blended.

Tap the grape to pick the splat that gives the most to that pixel.

2⁰ to 2¹⁸, then all

From one splat to all of them

Merge the grape's splats two at a time, keeping each pair's mass, center, and covariance, and it comes down to a single Gaussian.

Turn the dial to split it back up, one level at a time, and draw each splat as an ellipsoid, a ring, or a point.

Built from a recipe, on your device

Most of Splashery's scenes were never captured. A recipe of a few kilobytes says what an object is made of, and your device builds the splats, with a budget of 60,000 to 400,000. This is Splashery's own treasure chest recipe, run in this page.

Read the recipe

Change the wood or the budget, and it builds again.

one cloud, many parts

Moving parts inside one splat cloud

The lid is a part: a pivot and an axis in the recipe. Opening it turns those splats on the graphics chip, so nothing is rebuilt. Hinges, pivots, sliders, morphs, and per-splat effects all move this way.

x, y, z in u, v, t

Equations as a language

Type x, y, and z in u, v, and t, and every splat follows. Splashery's own reader parses it here: arithmetic only, no eval, and a plain message instead of a crash.

X-ray CT · 0.3 mm

Real data, as splats

Some of Splashery's science toys use measured data. This is a real walnut, scanned with X-ray CT at CWI in Amsterdam: more than a thousand X-ray pictures, worked back into a volume. Each splat is drawn from the volume's 0.3 mm voxels.

as far as we know

What we haven't seen elsewhere

As far as we know. We have not tried every splat tool.

  1. Toys made from rulesA recipe of a few kilobytes is built into splats on your device, with a budget of 60,000 to 400,000. The shape is never a large file to download.
  2. Equations as a languageType x, y, and z in u, v, and t, and get an animated splat shape, with plain messages instead of crashes.
  3. Moving parts in one splat cloudHinges, pivots, sliders, morphs, and per-splat effects run on the graphics chip, so a chest opens without rebuilding anything.
  4. Hands-on physicsToss, stack, pull apart, and pour, on splats.
  5. Anything as splatsPhotos and videos with depth, PDFs, QR codes, real science data, the night sky, and medical volumes.
  6. A whole scene in a linkThe link to the grape is 97 characters, and no server stores it.

Everything above ran in this tab.
